## Admission
- Last date: admission continues until the end of August.
- Online: apply at admission.iul.ac.in, fill the online application form and pay the application fee of ₹1,000.
- Offline: buy the application form from the university campus and submit it, with all information and documents, at the Directorate of Admissions and Academics.
- Counselors are available on campus to guide students.
- Documents required: High School and Intermediate marksheets/certificates (if result declared); Graduation marksheets (if applicable); Migration/Transfer Certificate; Character Certificate; Affidavit for gap period (if any); 5 latest coloured photographs; Caste Certificate (OBC/SC/ST candidates); Aadhaar Card; NAD id.

## Eligibility
- Minimum 50% marks is required for all courses.
- Subject-wise requirements (e.g. PCM for B.Tech) are NOT in the data: refer to the admission office.

## Fee rules
- "1st year fee" is the full first-year fee (both installments together).
- From the 2nd year to the last year, the fee is ₹10,000 less than the 1st year fee, same amount every year. This does not apply to M.B.B.S., M.D., M.S., 1-year courses, and courses whose fee is ₹10,000 or less.
- ₹5,000 rebate on one-time payment of the full fee.
- Polytechnic = the Diploma courses of University Polytechnic.

## Extra charges (in addition to tuition)
- M.B.B.S.: hostel ₹3,00,000; security ₹2,00,000 (one time, refundable); miscellaneous ₹1,00,000 per year.
- M.D. and M.S.: hostel (optional) ₹3,00,000; security ₹10,000 (one time); enrollment ₹10,000 (one time); miscellaneous ₹20,000 per year; examination ₹1,00,000 per year.
- Non-clinical medical programs (M.Sc. Medical Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Microbiology): hostel (shared) ₹2,60,000; security ₹1,00,000 (refundable); miscellaneous ₹1,00,000 per year.

## Hostel fee (per year, other programs)
- Non-AC room: Single ₹1,25,000; Double not available; Triple ₹1,00,000; security ₹5,000 (refundable).
- AC room: Single ₹1,75,000; Double not available; Triple ₹1,35,000; security ₹5,000 (refundable).
- ₹5,000 discount for girls.

## Scholarships (waiver on tuition fee only; terms and conditions apply)
- Merit based: 90% or more marks = 100% waiver; 85% or more = 50%; 80% or more = 25%.
- 25% waiver: Sibling of an enrolled student; Alumni or sibling of an alumnus; child/spouse of a staff member; applicants from Jammu & Kashmir; applicants from the North Eastern Region; Progression (pass-out students taking admission in the consecutive session in Bachelors, Masters or Ph.D.); Adopted Village (applicants from villages adopted by the university, girls only); Sports (Uttar Pradesh domicile plus recognition by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Govt. of India).
- 50% waiver: Specially Abled (at least 40% disability certified by a competent medical authority); Orphan (both parents no more).
- Integral Research Fellowship (IRF): 1st rank in the Ph.D. Entrance Test in every discipline gets ₹6,000 per month.
- Means Based Scholarship: financial aid for deserving students from financially underprivileged backgrounds.
